Is it safe?

Like any medical treatment, prescription weight-management medications can have side effects. The most common are digestive, such as nausea. Dr. Fortino reviews your health history first, explains what to expect, and uses follow-up visits to adjust your plan. Individual results vary, and treatment requires a medical evaluation by a licensed provider.

Who supervises my care?

Your care is led by Dr. Robert Fortino, DO, a board-certified internal medicine physician, with his clinical team, and follow-up takes place in person at our Philadelphia or Turnersville office.

Is this just medication?

No. Medication may be part of a plan when it's appropriate, alongside personalized nutrition and lifestyle guidance and regular follow-up. Individual results vary, and treatment requires a medical evaluation by a licensed provider.

What if it's not right for me?

Medication isn't right for everyone. GLP-1 medications are not recommended for some people, including those who are pregnant or planning a pregnancy, and people with a personal or family history of certain thyroid cancers. Dr. Fortino will go over this with you at your evaluation and talk through other options. Individual results vary, and treatment requires a medical evaluation by a licensed provider.
